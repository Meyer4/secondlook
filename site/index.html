A SECOND OPINION. NOT A SAFETY CERTIFICATE.

Meet SecondLook.

A small, open-source toolkit for the moments when something online feels a little off. Built to be useful, understandable, and private.

What the check actually does

It looks for a defined set of common English-language scam patterns and examines the structure of web addresses. It uses transparent rules, not an AI model or a live threat database.

It does not visit links, follow redirects, verify senders, scan files, or certify websites. It can miss scams and flag legitimate messages. A result with no warning signs is not a “safe” verdict.

Your information stays with you

Messages, links, and generated passwords are processed in this page’s memory. They are not uploaded, logged by the app, or saved as check history. Clear the check or close the page to remove it from the app.

Only your checklist progress is saved in this browser. The app has no analytics, ads, or third-party scripts. The hosting provider may log ordinary page and asset requests, but the app does not send your inputs with them.

Made to be accessible

No sign-up or paid API is required. After the hosted app’s first successful load and offline setup, it can work without a connection. Message patterns currently focus on English; messages in other languages or disguised wording may be missed.
